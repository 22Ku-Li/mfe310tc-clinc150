# CLINC150 attribution and dataset licensing

The CLINC150 dataset and derived textual examples and predictions in this repository originate from:

Stefan Larson, Anish Mahendran, Joseph J. Peper, Christopher Clarke, Andrew Lee, Parker Hill, Jonathan K. Kummerfeld, Kevin Leach, Michael A. Laurenzano, Lingjia Tang, and Jason Mars. “An Evaluation Dataset for Intent Classification and Out-of-Scope Prediction.” Proceedings of EMNLP-IJCNLP 2019, pp. 1311–1316. https://aclanthology.org/D19-1131/

Dataset source: UCI Machine Learning Repository, CLINC150, https://doi.org/10.24432/C5MP58 . The original research repository is https://github.com/clinc/oos-eval .

License information in the supplied material differs: UCI metadata identifies Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International, while the LICENSE file included in the downloaded UCI archive identifies Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported. The unmodified archive license text is retained as `CLINC150-ARCHIVE-LICENSE.txt`. Further verification of the applicable terms is recommended before redistribution. Raw and processed data are excluded from this repository; predictions and examples may include dataset text.

The dataset is not a manufacturing corpus. Attribution is required when the dataset or its material is reused; source texts and examples may have been transformed for the disclosed spelling-noise experiments.
